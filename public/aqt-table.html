AIDS Quilt Touch Table Exhibition

Interactive touch table exhibition bringing the entire AIDS Memorial Quilt to museums and galleries worldwide

Travelling Exhibition

The AQT Table is a large-scale interactive touch table exhibition that travels with physical blocks of the AIDS Memorial Quilt to museums and galleries worldwide. This innovative display adds a digital component to Quilt exhibitions, bringing the entire memorial along through cutting-edge technology.

Visitors can interact with a scalable, high-resolution image of the complete Quilt and explore the stories of 100,000+ individuals memorialized within its panels. The table helps convey the history, scale, and profound significance of this living memorial through immersive digital interactions.

Interactive Touch Table Experience

🖐️ Multi-Touch Interface: Navigate through the complete Quilt using intuitive gestures

🔍 Scalable Exploration: Zoom from overview of all 50,000+ panels to individual name details

📖 Rich Storytelling: Discover histories, connections, and personal stories behind each panel

🎵 Audio Integration: Listen to recorded names from the Call My Name project
